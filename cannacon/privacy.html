Privacy

Your visit. Your choices.

CannaCon uses essential browser storage for age acknowledgement and privacy preferences. External media may use third-party cookies when you choose to view it.

Age acknowledgement

We remember your 21+ acknowledgement for up to 180 days on the browser you use. Clearing browser storage or using a different browser may cause the question to appear again.

Privacy preferences

Essential storage remembers your age acknowledgement and privacy choices. When you choose to view external media such as YouTube, that provider may set its own cookies or process information under its own privacy terms.

Inquiries

When you use an inquiry form, your browser opens your email application with the information you entered so you can send it to CannaCon. Your email provider handles that message.
