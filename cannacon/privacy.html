Privacy, without the mystery.

This is a practical disclosure of what this test site does. A final production privacy notice requires owner review before launch.

Essential browser storage

We use a browser storage entry and a first-party cookie to remember a 21+ acknowledgement for 180 days. The cookie is scoped to this CannaCon folder. This is an acknowledgement, not identity or age verification. Clearing storage, using a different browser, expiry or private browsing may cause the question to reappear.

No analytics in this preview

No GA4, advertising pixels or optional analytics scripts are installed by these pages. The hosting provider may maintain its own operational logs.

External media and email inquiries

Approved photos and optional video are currently served by CannaCon.org. YouTube loads only when requested or when you have saved external-media permission. Inquiry pages prepare email drafts locally. No inquiry data is sent to a server by this preview. When you open a draft or send a message, your email provider handles that information. Copy and save controls provide an alternative to an installed email app.

Newsletter signup is a non-submitting prototype. Ticket selections are calculated in the browser; no checkout or card fields are connected and no ticket is issued.

Public preview

This preview and its linked PDFs are publicly reachable. Search-engine instructions are not authentication. Do not upload private customer data, credentials, confidential contracts or unapproved documents.
