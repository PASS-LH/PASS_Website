Level 1

Business Finance Foundations

READ → DIAGNOSE → CASH → DECIDE

Read the financial story of the business, connect operating performance to cash and make a forward decision.

  • Revenue, profit and cash
  • Unit economics and cost behavior
  • Sell-through and inventory
  • Cash forecasting
  • Pricing and volume decisions

Level 2

Financial Management & Analytics

PRIORITIZE → ALLOCATE → CONTROL → ADAPT

Determine where scarce operating resources should go and how management should know when the plan is drifting.

  • Contribution economics
  • Constraints and opportunity cost
  • Resource allocation
  • Demand headroom
  • KPIs, variance and scenarios

Program FAQ

Know what you are buying.

Do I need finance or accounting experience?

No formal finance training is required to start with Level 1. Direct-entry participants in Levels 2 or 3 should already be comfortable with foundational business-finance concepts.

Can I take one level without the others?

Yes. Each 2.5-hour applied level is standalone. The one-hour overview seminar is also a separate offering.

Do the levels have to be taken in order?

No. Level 1 is the foundation, while Levels 2 and 3 can be selected independently by participants with the prerequisite fluency.

Is the $500 program a one-day bundle?

No. The $500 price covers all three 2.5-hour applied levels. Scheduling may span the available event education windows or separate offering dates.

What is required for the professional certificate?

Complete all three competency areas, complete the integrated capstone, document substantive AI use where applicable, defend the work in the review conversation and meet the stated completion standard.

Does the one-hour seminar count as an applied level?

No. It is an overview offering and is separate from the three applied competency levels.

Does course registration include CannaCon admission?

No. Education and CannaCon show-floor admission are separate products in the current CannaCon checkout.
