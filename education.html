Program Rules

Flexible by design.

  • Each applied level can stand alone.
  • Levels do not need to be taken sequentially.
  • Levels may be completed at different event dates.
  • A test-out pathway may be available for foundational requirements when pursuing the full certificate.
  • The professional certificate is issued only by Play At Scale.
  • It is not an academic degree, accredited academic credential, professional license, or governmental certification.
  • CannaCon exhibitor discounts may be available through CannaCon sales and are not administered by Play At Scale.
  • Custom online education may be considered by inquiry; private in-person programs are not currently offered.
Instructor

Complex finance, translated for the people who have to use it.

Lucas Hobbs has more than 13 years of experience teaching finance to non-technical audiences alongside senior finance, strategy, analytics, and transformation roles.

  • Developed and administered Finance for Non-Technical Audiences at Boeing.
  • Received the 2014 Ed Wells Program - Professional Instructor Excellence Award, selected from an instructor pool of approximately 300.
  • Course content was later adopted by the University of Washington and administered for five years as part of Boeing / UW professional development certificate programming.
  • Teaching approach emphasizes practical application, decision structure, and meeting participants at their existing level of financial fluency.
