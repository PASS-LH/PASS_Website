What participants learn to decide

Finance matters when it changes the decision.

The program starts with business questions, then introduces the finance needed to answer them. The goal is not accounting vocabulary for its own sake. It is better financial stewardship.

Cash

Revenue is up. Why is cash down?

Connect profit, inventory, collections, payroll, debt, capital spending and cash timing.

Build a forward view of where cash is actually going.
Operations

Which product should get the next $50,000?

Use contribution, constraints, demand headroom, sell-through and opportunity cost to allocate scarce resources.

Put cash, labor, inventory or capacity where it creates the most value.
Capital

The project has a positive return. Should we fund it?

Build project cash flows, test NPV and IRR, stress assumptions, check liquidity and compare competing uses of capital.

Determine whether the project is actually financeable and worth funding.

FAQ

What to know before you register.

Who is this for?

Owners, operators, managers, advisors and other decision-makers who need to use financial information to run the business. It is designed for non-accountants as well as financially experienced operators.

Do I need finance or accounting experience?

No formal prerequisite is required. Level 1 establishes the common financial language. Participants entering directly at Levels 2 or 3 may receive short readiness work so the live session can stay focused on the higher-level decisions.

Can I take one level without the others?

Yes. Each applied level can stand alone. The levels are cumulative when taken as a pathway, but direct entry is allowed when the participant has the needed foundation.

How long is each session?

The open overview lecture is approximately one hour. Each applied level is a four-hour live instructor-led session. Levels 2 and 3 may include short readiness work for direct-entry participants.

What tools are included?

Applicable sessions include participant workbooks and financial decision tools such as cash forecasting, unit economics, constrained-resource allocation, KPI/variance, scenarios and capital-allocation models. Public download links appear only when a participant-ready asset is available.

What is required to earn the Play At Scale professional certificate?

The certificate pathway requires the required competency areas plus the integrated applied capstone and a 15-minute review presentation. The capstone may use supplied case data, the participant's own business data, or a hybrid.

Is the certificate accredited?

No. It is a private professional certificate issued by Play At Scale. It is not an academic degree, accredited academic credential, professional license, governmental certification or independent personnel certification.

Can I use my own business data for the capstone?

Yes. Participants may anonymize products, customers and other sensitive details or consistently scale dollar values. The requirement is a coherent, internally consistent model that demonstrates the same competencies as the supplied case.

Is AI allowed?

Yes, and thoughtful use is encouraged. Participants document substantive prompts, remain responsible for validation and judgment, and defend the analysis during the 15-minute review presentation.

Does course registration include CannaCon admission?

CannaCon admission rules are controlled by the applicable event registration. Use the current CannaCon course registration page for the event-specific rule before purchasing.

Are exhibitor discounts or group registrations available?

CannaCon exhibitor discounts may be available through CannaCon sales. For group registration or invoice needs, contact Play At Scale or the applicable event registration team for the current options.

What is the cancellation or transfer policy?

The policy shown by the applicable registration provider at checkout governs the purchase. Review those terms before completing registration.
