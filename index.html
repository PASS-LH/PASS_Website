Frequently asked questions

Straight answers before you register or engage.

Who is the program designed for?

Founders, owner-operators, managers, and business leaders who make financial decisions but do not need a finance degree. The material begins with practical fundamentals and progresses into analytics, capital, and strategic finance.

Do the levels have to be taken in order?

No. Each level stands on its own and can be taken on different offering dates. Participants pursuing the full Play At Scale professional certificate may also be eligible to test out of earlier foundational levels.

What is the difference between the lecture and the level sessions?

The open lecture is an approximately one-hour overview designed for a larger audience of roughly 300 people. Each level is a smaller instructor-led applied session capped at 50 participants and includes deeper tools, cases, and practical frameworks.

What do I receive if I complete the full pathway?

Participants who complete all three levels, or satisfy approved test-out requirements where applicable, can receive a professional certificate issued by Play At Scale. It is a provider-issued professional-development credential and does not represent academic credit, licensure, or third-party accreditation.

Is the content cannabis-specific?

The current program is delivered in an industry-relevant context at CannaCon, but the underlying finance, pricing, cash-flow, analytics, capital, and strategy frameworks are broadly applicable across industries.

Are private or online sessions available?

Private in-person delivery is not currently offered. Custom online sessions may be considered for organizations with a defined need; contact Play At Scale to discuss scope and availability.

Can exhibitors receive discounted pricing?

CannaCon may offer exhibitor-specific pricing or discounts. Those arrangements are administered through CannaCon sales rather than directly through Play At Scale.

Does Play At Scale provide advisory work outside the program?

Yes. Advisory work can focus on business finance, capital decisions, commercial negotiations, pricing, contract risk, analytics, management systems, and decision support. Engagements are scoped around a defined decision or business problem.
