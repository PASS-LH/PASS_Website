Illustrative / Sanitized

Product Portfolio Economics

Problem
Volume, package mix, pricing, and allocated costs can make high-selling products look healthier than their true unit economics suggest.
Analysis
Connect product mix, sell-through, package-level costs, logistics, overhead allocation, and net margin in one operating view.
Decision Framework
Separate unit economics from total contribution so leadership can see which products move volume, fund overhead, and generate profit.
Business Use
Pricing, product mix, package strategy, cost visibility, margin improvement, and portfolio planning.
Illustrative / Sanitized

Product Portfolio Strategy

Problem
High demand does not always mean high value, and high-value products may be constrained by market demand rather than production economics.
Analysis
Compare demand with contribution per gram after variable costs and logistics, while keeping fixed overhead allocation separate from product-level decisions.
Decision Framework
Segment the portfolio into products to optimize, scale, rationalize, or develop based on both market pull and economic value.
Business Use
Capacity allocation, assortment strategy, demand development, format conversion, and product rationalization.
